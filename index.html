How it works

A calm clipboard, on call.

Five quick notes on what Enhance Clipboard does, what it remembers, and what it leaves alone.

01 Access

Open it from anywhere.

A single shortcut brings up your clipboard wherever you're typing—no menu hunting, no app switching.

02 Keyboard

Stay on the keys.

Arrows to browse, Enter to paste, Esc to dismiss. Your hands never leave the keyboard.

03 Memory

Text and images, remembered.

Snippets, links, and screenshots—the beta keeps the things you actually reach for again.

04 Organize

Pin what matters. Clear the rest.

Important clips stay on top. Old history disappears with a single keystroke when the list gets noisy.

05 Privacy

Yours, on your Mac.

History stays local. No account, no sync, no servers—and free on Apple Silicon.
